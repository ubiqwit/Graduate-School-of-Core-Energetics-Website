Welcome

Therapy that includes your whole body

Most talk therapy asks you to describe your experience. This work asks something different: to notice it, in real time, as it moves through you. Tension in the jaw. A held breath. The urge to look away. These aren't distractions from the work — they often are the work.

If you've done years of insight-oriented therapy and still feel stuck in the same patterns, or if you're simply curious what it might mean to bring your body into the room, this is a place to start.

The Approach

What is Core Energetics?

Core Energetics is a body-oriented psychotherapy that grew out of psychoanalytic and bioenergetic traditions. It works from a simple premise: emotional experience isn't only stored in memory or thought — it's held in posture, breathing patterns, muscle tension, and the way energy moves (or doesn't) through the body.

In session, we combine conversation with attention to what's happening physically — breath, sensation, impulse, and expression — to help longstanding defenses soften and more authentic feeling and self-expression become available. The goal isn't catharsis for its own sake, but a more honest, grounded relationship with yourself and the people in your life.

Four Facets of the Work

How this shows up in a session

Grounding

Building a felt connection to your body and the present moment as a foundation for everything else.

Charge & Discharge

Learning how energy and tension move through you, and how blocked energy shows up as familiar, stuck patterns.

Embodied Awareness

Treating sensation, breath, and posture as information worth listening to, not obstacles to reason past.

Authentic Expression

Practicing the emotional honesty that's often been armored over, for good reason, long ago.

FAQ

Common questions

I've never done body-based therapy. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most clients start here with no prior experience. We build the skill of noticing your body gradually, and you're never asked to do anything you're not ready for.

Do I need to be comfortable with movement or physical touch?

No. This work can include movement, breath, and sound, but nothing physical happens without your explicit consent, and plenty of clients do this work through awareness and conversation alone.

How is this different from regular talk therapy?

We still talk — a lot. The difference is that we also track what's happening in your body as you talk, which often surfaces material that words alone don't reach.
